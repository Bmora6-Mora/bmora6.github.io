Travel Tips at a Glance

Use this quick guide to plan safer and smoother days while exploring Mexico.

Quick travel guidance for safer, smarter days in Mexico
Category Tip Best Time to Apply Cost Impact
Transportation Use authorized taxis or ride-share pickups at airports. Arrival and late evenings Medium savings, safer rides
Money Carry small cash bills for markets and local buses. Daily outings Avoids overpaying and extra fees
Health Stay hydrated and choose sealed bottled water. All day, especially warm weather Low cost, high comfort
Packing Pack light layers, sunscreen, and a reusable day bag. Before departure Reduces extra luggage costs
Safety Share itinerary details and avoid isolated areas at night. Before and during each day No cost, strong risk reduction
